1

Opening story: "The notice board"

45 secondsSlides or no screen

Meet Amina. She's 19 and lives in Kakuma, in northwest Kenya. She finished secondary school with good grades, and she dreams of studying engineering.

One morning, a friend forwards her a WhatsApp message: a scholarship for students just like her. She's excited, until she reads the date. The deadline was last week. pause

The information existed. It was pinned on a notice board on the other side of the camp, posted in a group she wasn't in, written in a language she reads slowly.

And somewhere in Germany, Daniel, who grew up in Kakuma and is now a software engineer, wants to help someone like Amina. He just has no way to find her.

Amina didn't miss that opportunity because she wasn't good enough. She missed it because the information never reached her. slow down

That's why we built Kakuma ResourceLink: one place where opportunities, services, stories and mentors find the people who need them, in their own language, even on a weak connection.

Let me show you Amina's journey, the way it should have gone.

Short version (20 seconds, if time is tight)

Amina is 19, lives in Kakuma, and dreams of studying engineering. Last year she missed a scholarship because she heard about it a week after the deadline. It was on a notice board across the camp and in a WhatsApp group she wasn't in. Meanwhile Daniel, a Kakuma alumnus in Germany, wanted to mentor someone like her but couldn't find her. Kakuma ResourceLink connects them. Let me show you how.

Tips for telling it
  • Learn the beats, not the words: Amina's dream → the missed deadline → why she missed it → Daniel who wants to help → the platform.
  • Pause after "The deadline was last week" and let it land.
  • Look at the judges, not the screen. This part needs no slides.
  • If asked whether Amina is real: "She's a composite, based on how information really moves in Kakuma."
2

Browse as a guest

30 secondsWeb · Window A
  • Show the home page: the hero, the pillars, open opportunities and recent stories.
  • Point out the "Unverified" / sample labels on listings.

Anyone can browse without an account. And we never pass off unverified information as fact: every listing shows whether our team has checked it.

3

Find an opportunity, explained by AI

1 minuteWeb · Window A · Amina
  • Sign in as Amina. Show the short "Welcome back" toast and her dashboard.
  • Opportunities → filter by Scholarship → point at the deadline chip ("30 days left").
  • Bookmark one (toast: "Saved"), open it, click Explain with AI.

The assistant explains the listing in plain words, using only what is published on the platform. It shows its sources, and it never decides whether you're eligible: that stays with the organisation.

4

Same account on the phone, in Kiswahili, offline

1 minuteAndroid
  • Sign in on the phone as Amina. Open saved opportunities: the bookmark from the web is already there.
  • Profile → Language → Kiswahili: the whole app switches.
  • Turn on airplane mode: saved opportunities and stories still open, and the offline banner appears. Turn it off again.

One account, one backend, one database: what Amina saves on the web is on her phone. The app speaks five languages and keeps working when the connection drops, which in Kakuma is often.

5

Ask a mentor

1 minuteWeb · Windows A and B
  • Window A (Amina): Alumni → Daniel M. (software engineer, Germany) → Request mentorship, pick "Career", add a short message. Toast: "Request sent".
  • Window B: sign in as Daniel → Mentorship → Received → Accept.
  • Back in Window A: the notification bell shows the update and links straight to the request.

Former residents around the world can mentor people in Kakuma. Alumni choose what to share, and can hide their profile at any time.

6

Share a story

1 minuteAndroidor Web
  • Write a story: a title, a few lines, a cover photo, and the consent box.
  • Point out the checklist and the privacy tip ("don't share case numbers").
  • Mention that drafts save automatically, so a dropped connection doesn't lose the work.
  • Submit for review: the status becomes In review.

Stories inspire the next generation, but they can also expose people. So every story is reviewed before it goes public, and the editor warns against sharing case numbers or other identifying details.

7

Moderation

45 secondsWeb · Window B · Moderator
  • Window B: sign out of Daniel, sign in as the moderator.
  • Moderation → Stories → open Amina's story → Approve. It's now public, and Amina is notified in her own language.

Rejecting a story requires a kind, specific reason, and only the author sees it. Every moderator action is recorded in an audit log.

8

Give back

45 secondsWeb · Window A · Amina
  • Community → Solar study lamps → Pledge support → Volunteering.
  • The confirmation says "Pledged (not a payment)".

People can pledge time, skills or support to community projects. No money moves through the platform: a pledge is a commitment, and the project owner follows up directly.

This time, Amina found the scholarship before the deadline, got a mentor in Germany, and shared her own story to inspire the next student. Five languages, offline-first, the same account on web and Android, and AI that only speaks from what's published on the platform. It's live today: the web app is on Vercel, the API on Render, and anyone can download the Android app from our GitHub. If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
Pages load slowly or the app says "No connection"The free server is waking up. Say "it's on free hosting, it's waking up", wait 30–60 seconds, and refresh. Next time, open the health check first.
"The assistant is busy" or "not available"Gemini is briefly overloaded, or the account hit 20 AI questions this hour. Try again in a moment or switch to another account. Explain that the assistant refuses rather than making things up.
"Request mentorship" is greyed out or says already requestedAmina was used in a rehearsal. Request a different mentor, or sign in as another member.
The notification bell hasn't updatedNotifications are checked every minute. Refresh the page; on the phone, reopen the app.
"Session expired" on the webSign in again. Sign-in tokens rotate for security, so an old tab may hold one that was already used.
A photo won't uploadSkip the photo and continue; the story works without one. Check Render's Logs afterwards.
The internet at the venue fails completelyPlay the recorded walkthrough (download it beforehand), or run everything locally on your laptop as described in the README.

Likely judge questions

How do you stop the AI making things up?For each question we retrieve matching published listings and give only those to the model, with strict instructions. It cites its sources, never judges eligibility or refugee status, and says it doesn't know rather than guessing.
What about privacy?Stories are reviewed before publishing, the editor warns against case numbers, alumni profiles are opt-in and can be hidden, and the AI can run entirely on our own server with Ollama so chats never leave it. The live demo uses Gemini's free tier for convenience.
How does it work on weak connections?The Android app stores opportunities and stories in a local database, queues bookmarks until it reconnects, and saves story drafts automatically. The web app caches data and works as an installable app.
Is the information real?The demo data is fictional and labelled as samples. Before a real launch, listings would be verified with organisations working in Kakuma.
Why not just a WhatsApp group?Messages get buried, nothing is searchable or filterable, there's no moderation, no deadlines, no language switching and no link to alumni mentors.
